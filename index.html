Objective

Steal the enemy's flag and return it to your base. First team to reach the score limit wins.

Controls

  • W A S D or ↑←↓→ — Move
  • Mouse — Aim
  • Left Click or Space — Throw flag (when carrying)
  • Shift — Sprint (drains stamina)
  • E — Taunt
  • Esc — Pause / Menu

Rules

  • The field is split by a halfway line. While on the enemy's side, you can be tagged.
  • Around each base is a safe zone — players inside ANY safe zone (their own or the enemy's) cannot be tagged. This gives invaders a window to grab the flag.
  • When tagged, you respawn at your base after a short delay.
  • The flag carrier is slower than other players. Pass the flag to a teammate to move faster.
  • Aim with the mouse and click/press Space to throw the flag. Teammates catch it — enemies intercept it.
  • If the enemy touches their own dropped flag, it returns to their base.
  • Carry the enemy flag to your base to score. Your fanfare plays and your banner shows for everyone.
